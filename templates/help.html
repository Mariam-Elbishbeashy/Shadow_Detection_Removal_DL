Frequently Asked Questions

How does ShadowRemove's AI technology work?

ShadowRemove uses advanced deep learning models trained on thousands of images to detect and remove shadows intelligently. Our AI analyzes the lighting, texture, and color patterns in your image to identify shadow regions and seamlessly blend them with the surrounding areas for a natural-looking result.

What image formats are supported?

ShadowRemove supports all common image formats including JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WEBP. For best results, we recommend using high-quality images with a resolution between 1-10MP. The maximum file size allowed is 20MB.

How long does it take to process an image?

Processing time depends on the image size and complexity. Typically, images are processed within 10-30 seconds. Larger images or those with complex shadows may take up to a minute. You can monitor the progress in real-time on the dashboard.

Is there a limit to how many images I can process?

Accounts can process unlimited images.

How do I get the best results with ShadowRemove?

For optimal results:

  • Use images with good lighting contrast
  • Ensure shadows are clearly defined
  • Avoid images with extremely complex shadows or overlapping objects
